# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Agent Flow community a
welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive environment includes:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward others
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks, publishing
others' private information without permission, and any other conduct which could reasonably be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer,
[@AleSaiani](https://github.com/AleSaiani). All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
